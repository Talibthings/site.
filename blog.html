How to pretend: an essay

Organisms behave pretentiously, consciously or unconsciously. Organisms perform activity. Activity is derived of abstractions, abstraction are created with combination of binaries.

In order for an activity to take place, components and factors has to take place in order to cause activity. The way computer programs are created by programing languages, and programing languages are representation of binary, the raw 0s and 1s.

Extending the example, computer program if the activity, programing languages are components and factors, and 0s and 1s are binary.

Activities and behaviours are very same, one cannot exist without the other. As we extrapolated that activities are derived out components and factors. So does behaviours.

In order to pretend, one has to behave. Pretentiousness is a behaviour. To be pretencious one has to choose components and factors or one can go further down to choose binary.(albeit author doesn't believe there's freewill, but they chose the word 'choose' for the sake of language.)

Although, one has to choose the pretenciousmess itself, that denotes to how one wants to pretend. Then one may accumulate components and factors accordingly, or the binary itself.

Albeit there's no freewill, so anyway organisms are being pretencious all the time, unless they are not performing activity, which organisms don't do. Pretentiousness is status quo and it cannot be altered by organisms.
